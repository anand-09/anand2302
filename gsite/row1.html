Hospital: 6 delivery robots

Six delivery robots share narrow hospital corridors with walking people, beds, and wheelchairs. Each robot slows down and keeps a safe distance when someone comes close, even when its sensors cannot see around corners. This is the setting for my PhD work on safe reinforcement learning.

Simulation · Institute of Science Tokyo

Warehouse: 4 transport robots

Four transport robots move goods between shelves while workers and forklifts move around them. Because every robot’s choice affects the others, I use game theory to plan their motion so they take turns at crossings instead of blocking or hitting each other.
