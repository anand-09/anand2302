Hidden attacks on learning robots

A learning robot trusts what its sensors tell it. A hidden attack makes small changes to those inputs that are almost impossible to detect, yet the robot moves very differently. Choose a robot to compare a normal run with one under hidden attack; understanding these attacks is the first step to robots that resist them.

Voice-controlled robot car

A four-wheel robot that drives when you talk to it. Commands such as “move forward”, “turn left” or “stop” go from a phone app over Wi-Fi to an Arduino, and an ultrasonic sensor on a turning servo looks around for obstacles. I showed it to visitors at the IISc Open Day.
