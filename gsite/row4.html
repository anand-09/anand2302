TurtleBot 4

I set up the TurtleBot 4 during my M.Tech. for indoor mapping and navigation with ROS 2. The video shows how it builds a map of a room with its lidar as it drives, which it then uses to plan safe paths.

Aerial robot

Crazyflie 2.1

The Crazyflie 2.1 is a small indoor drone that I used for flight control and team flying experiments. Its light weight makes it safe to test new controllers indoors, close to people and other robots.
